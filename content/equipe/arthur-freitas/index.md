I hold a degree in Physics and am currently a graduate student in Nuclear Science and Technology at the Federal University of Minas Gerais (UFMG), Brazil. My research spans the fields of Statistical Physics and Geospatial Engineering. I have conducted research on stochastic differential equations, heat flow in oscillator chains with stochastic baths, and geospatial modeling for renewable energy using QGIS and PyQGIS. My work analyzing solar potential on urban rooftops in Belo Horizonte was recognized with two "Academic Relevance" awards at UFMG's 34th Scientific Initiation Week. My skills include computational modeling, data analysis, and scientific methodology. I am fluent in Portuguese and proficient in English.
