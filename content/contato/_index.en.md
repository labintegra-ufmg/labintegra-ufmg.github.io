## How to get involved

We welcome new students and collaborations.

- **Undergraduate research:** follow UFMG's research-initiation calls and write to us with your background and interests.
- **Master's and PhD:** admission goes through the PCTN selection process. Please contact us first to align your interests with our research lines.
- **Partnerships and projects:** institutions, companies and public agencies interested in collaborating are welcome.

When writing, please include your name, affiliation, area of interest and a link to your CV (Lattes, ORCID or LinkedIn).
