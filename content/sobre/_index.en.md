title: "About the lab"
---
**LabIntegra** is a laboratory of the School of Engineering at the Federal University of Minas Gerais (UFMG) devoted to energy, energy planning, renewable sources and the low-carbon energy transition. It is linked to the Graduate Program in Nuclear Science and Technology and Energy Engineering (PCTN).

We bring together professors, researchers, PhD, master's and undergraduate research students around a central question: how can we integrate different sources, technologies and sectors to build a reliable, affordable and low-carbon energy system?

## Mission

To produce scientific knowledge and train people able to plan and operate sustainable energy systems, supporting decisions by society, industry and government.

## Vision

To be a national reference in integrated energy planning and the low-carbon energy transition, recognized for scientific quality, human-resource training and engagement with society.

## What we do

- Long-term energy system modeling and planning.
- Assessment and grid integration of renewable sources.
- Analysis of technological pathways and policies for decarbonization, including hydrogen and nuclear power.
- Studies on storage, flexibility and cross-sector integration.

## How we work

- **Interdisciplinarity:** electrical, nuclear and energy engineering, economics and the environment come together in every project.
- **Open science:** the publication list on this site is updated automatically from ORCID and OpenAlex, and we favor open-access work.
- **Training:** every student takes part in real projects, with close supervision and encouragement to publish and collaborate.
